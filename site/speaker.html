In Chad's words

Technology matters most when it helps people do their best work.

My career has kept me close to the intersection of people, technology, and change. I am interested not only in what a new tool can do, but in how teams understand it, make good decisions with it, and turn possibility into durable progress.

When I speak, I aim to make complex ideas approachable without sanding away the details that matter. The best sessions feel like the start of a useful conversation—one that continues after everyone leaves the room.

Event-ready biography

About Chad

Chad Hage is a technologist and technical leader who explores how AI, developer tools, and modern engineering practices can help people and organizations do better work. He brings a practical, approachable perspective to emerging technology, enterprise change, and the human side of technical leadership. Chad speaks with curiosity, clarity, and a focus on ideas audiences can carry into their own teams.
